The Studio

One team for design and execution.

Building a new home involves hundreds of decisions. Skylona brings interior design, civil work and on-site execution together, so you work with one team that understands both how a space should look and how it will actually be built.

Our style leans towards modern luxury — warm neutral palettes, natural materials like wood, stone and cane, soft lighting and uncluttered layouts that suit Kerala homes and the way families live in them.

Considered design

Layouts planned around your family's routines, light and ventilation.

Natural materials

Warm textures and finishes chosen to age well in Kerala's climate.

How We Work

A clear path from idea to handover.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We understand your home, family, budget range and timeline — over WhatsApp, a call or a meeting.

  2. 02

    Site & Plan Review

    We study your house plan or site and discuss the scope of work and requirements.

  3. 03

    Design & 3D

    Layouts, materials and 3D views are prepared and refined together with you.

  4. 04

    Execution

    Our team carries out civil and interior work, keeping you updated as the home takes shape.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Final finishing, a walkthrough with you, and your new space is ready to live in.
